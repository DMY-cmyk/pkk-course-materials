5. **Dua Metode Penyajian: Direct vs Indirect** (R pp.3–4) — direct vs indirect method; FASB favors direct but it is costlier; indirect easier but harder to understand; "plug number" in practice; direct requires the indirect reconciliation as a supplement; majority of US firms use indirect; McEnroe 282-respondent survey 56%/44%; FASB members fear indirect impedes understanding. Includes Exhibit 13.4 (3M) table.
7. **Masalah Klasifikasi dalam SFAS No. 95** (R pp.6–7) — proprietary income-statement orientation putting interest/dividend items in operating; finance-literature/entity view; banking-industry pressure to avoid negative operating cash; IAS 7 flexible-but-consistent approach; bond premium/discount problem; Vent, Cowling & Sevalstad four methods; authors' preference for Method 1; SFAS No. 34 capitalized interest and lease (capital vs operating) nature-vs-purpose issue; SFAS No. 104 hedge flexibility; Nurnberg & Largay "kehalusan"/fineness vs comparability. Includes Exhibit 13.5 (premium allocation, four methods) table.
8. **Kegunaan Analitis SCF dan Manipulasi Klasifikasi** (R pp.7–8) — Ingram & Lee growing-vs-contracting firms (higher income/lower operating cash when growing); ~1,000 firms 1974–1992; classification manipulation by shifting flows operating↔investing; Tyco/Maremont purchased-contracts example; automotive (Ford, GM, Harley-Davidson) dealer-loan example with GM $7.6B vs $3.5B.
9. **SCF Lebih dari Sekadar Arus Kas Operasi: Pelajaran dari WorldCom** (R p.8) — undue focus on CFO is dangerous; WorldCom July 2002 Chapter 11; capitalized expenses inflating CFO; positive net income three years pre-bankruptcy; CFO−CFI cumulative $12 billion hole by 12/31/01. Includes Exhibit 13.6 (WorldCom) table.
10. **Kebutuhan Arus Kas Berbagai Pengguna** (R p.9) — Buffett 1988 BRKA letter three key questions; valuation/credit/managerial-performance information needs; investment as prediction of future cash flows; intrinsic value vs market price; Buffett intrinsic-value definition; leads into FCF.
11. **Free Cash Flow (FCF)** (R p.9) — FCF = NOPLAT − investment in operating invested capital; NOPLAT = after-tax operating income; FCF excludes interest expense; operating cash treated as part of net operating working capital; FCF not obtainable directly from SCF; how to bridge CFO→FCF; the four-measure comparison (net income, CFO, CFO−CFI, FCF) and WACC discounting for intrinsic value. Includes Exhibit 13.9 (Statement of Free Cash Flows, ABC) table.
12. **Penelitian Empiris tentang Arus Kas dan Dana** (R pp.10–11) — Lawson & Lee advocacy; Lee "kas adalah sumber daya fisik, laba adalah abstraksi"; cash-flow valuation model; capital-market research that accruals add information beyond cash flows; cash+accruals complementary; FAF survey (funds-flow data rising, accrual data declining in importance).
13. **Rekomendasi Perbaikan SCF** (R p.11) — Broome critique of SFAS No. 95 flexibility; Broome's three recommendations (require direct + reconciliation; more classification guidance; reverse reconciliation order CFO→net income); authors' own recommendations (agree direct should be required; otherwise require a non-cash working-capital schedule; mid-year-acquisition reconciliation schedule; explain any nonarticulation).
14. **Kesimpulan** (R p.11) — change from SCFP to SCF brought greater consistency/comparability and predictive usefulness; SCF lacks the income statement's "kesewenang-wenangan"; remaining weaknesses (nonarticulation, classification flexibility); read the SCF in its entirety across all three sections.

## Ringkasan strengths to preserve

- **Faithful, well-ordered narrative spine.** The ringkasan follows the chapter's section order exactly (history → motivation → objectives → structure → methods → nonarticulation → classification → analytical usefulness → WorldCom → user needs → FCF → research → recommendations → conclusion). Keep this spine wholesale.
- **Good Indonesian terminology choices, consistently applied.** Keep these renderings: "modal kerja" (working capital), "laporan keuangan turunan / derivative" (derivative statement), "metode langsung / tidak langsung" (direct/indirect), "kualitas laba" with the English *quality of income* in parentheses, "fleksibilitas keuangan" (financial flexibility), "likuiditas" (liquidity), "angka plug" for plug number, "kehalusan" for *fineness*, "kas adalah kas" for "cash is cash is cash."
- **Bilingual term-on-first-use convention.** The ringkasan italicizes the English term in parentheses on first mention (*funds flow statement*, *cash equivalents*, *Free Cash Flows*). This is good academic style for a graduate RMK — preserve it.
- **Clean topic-sentence transitions.** Sections open with orienting sentences ("Untuk memahami SCF secara penuh…", "Meskipun memiliki berbagai masalah, SCF tetap…", "Namun jika dilihat secara menyeluruh…"). These narrative hinges are worth keeping verbatim where the enrichment doesn't displace them.
- **Already-reproduced exhibit tables (13.2, 13.3, 13.4, 13.5, 13.6, 13.9).** These are correctly transcribed from the chapter; preserve them as the visual backbone of the RMK.
- **Faithful preservation of key numbers** where given (McEnroe 56%/44%; 75% nonarticulation; 3M 300%; GM $7.6B vs $3.5B; WorldCom $12B hole). No number in the ringkasan contradicts the notes.
