## Assignment Question (verbatim)

Note: "FSAS" in the assignment image is a typo for **SFAS** (Statement of Financial Accounting Standards). The correct standard names are SFAS No. 131 (1997) and SFAS No. 14 (1976).

## Interpretation
The question asks the student to explain **why** SFAS 131's segment disclosure regime is an **improvement** over SFAS 14's. This is an analytical/argumentative essay requiring:
1. Characterization of SFAS 14's approach and its limitations
2. Characterization of SFAS 131's approach and its innovations
3. Causal argument for why the change constitutes an improvement
4. Empirical evidence supporting the improvement claim
